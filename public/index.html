Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about gameplay, controls, cloud sync, and family safety.

How does the drawing mechanic work? ✕
Simply touch or click anywhere on the screen and drag to draw a line bridge. Your line becomes an active, solid 2D rigid physics body that your toy car can drive across. Watch your ink bar at the top — drawing shorter, more clever bridges earns you a full 3-star rating!
Is the game free to play? +
Yes! Car Rush: Draw & Drive is completely free to download and play. All levels can be unlocked naturally through gameplay and star progression without requiring any paywalls.
How does Cloud Sync work? +
We use Firebase Firestore to sync your level completion and earned stars. A unique, anonymous pseudonymous device ID is assigned to your device on launch. When you are connected to Wi-Fi or mobile data, your progress syncs in real-time. If you are offline, all progress is cached locally and safely synced when reconnected.
Is Car Rush safe for young children? +
Absolutely. Car Rush: Draw & Drive is designed to be 100% family friendly and complies strictly with the Google Play Families Policy and the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We never collect names, photos, or personal messages. All advertisements served are restricted to G-rated content with child-directed flags enabled.
How can I request my data to be deleted? +
You have complete ownership over your data. You can submit a deletion request anytime directly on this website using our or by emailing us at info@appstick.com.bd. All records tied to your device key will be permanently purged within 48 hours.
